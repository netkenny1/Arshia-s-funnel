# 04 — Getting the crowd he actually wants

The ask was "attractive people who club". Every venue in Dubai wants the same thing and none of them get it with a database. They get it with positioning, friction and the door. Here's what works, in order of leverage.

## 1. The content casts the room

People show up to the night that looks like them. If every video is a well-dressed, mixed, high-energy crowd shot at 1am, that is who comes. If it's empty-floor soundcheck clips, that is who comes.
- Shoot the crowd, not the decks, 70% of the time.
- Shoot after midnight. Shoot the moment, not the room.
- Photographer or a friend with a phone every night: 30 photos, posted next morning, everyone tagged. Being in the dump becomes the reason to come.
- No "free entry" in the hook. "On the list" is the hook. Free is for everyone; the list is for someone.

## 2. Friction is the filter

- Guest list requires an Instagram handle (the form does). Nobody is scored by an algorithm; the door team glances at it like every club in Dubai already does, and a dead account with no photos doesn't get the free slot. That is the venue's existing policy, not a new system.
- Cut-off time. "List closes 9pm, arrive by 11:30." People who plan show up dressed; people who don't, don't.
- Group size on the form. Groups of 3–6 with mixed company are what venues want; the door can prioritise them.
- Dress code stated three times: on the page, in the confirmation, in the day-of message.

## 3. Seed, don't scrape

- **10 creators** (playbook 03). Pick from Dubai's lifestyle/fashion/fitness micro scene. Invite personally, +3 each. Their presence and their posts recruit their followers.
- **Agencies and communities, not individuals:** model agencies' social teams, cabin-crew group admins, university alumni groups, padel and run clubs, fashion boutique staff. One message to a community admin beats 200 cold DMs, and it isn't creepy.
- **Ladies-first list.** Both venues already run ladies' promotions; a "girls' list: +1 drink before midnight" line in the Tuesday WhatsApp message is standard in Dubai and shapes the room more than anything else on this page.

## 4. The night is the ad

- Shout-out a table, not a sponsor.
- A photographer who asks before shooting and sends the photo via DM next day. That DM converts to a follow at 50%+.
- One "moment" per set that's engineered to be filmed: a known drop, a crowd sing-along, a brief lights-off. That's the clip that gets shared, and the clip decides next week's room.

## 5. What not to do

- Buying followers or using engagement pods: TikTok/IG detect it, reach collapses for weeks.
- Mass cold DMs from his account: Instagram action-blocks at ~50/day and the people you want never answer them.
- "Free drinks for girls" as the headline online: it attracts the opposite of the target and venues hate it on record. Say it on WhatsApp, not on TikTok.
- Any tool that rates people. See the note in playbook 03.
