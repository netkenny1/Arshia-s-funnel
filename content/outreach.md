## Venue: resident listing + collab post
Subject: arshia.wav – resident listing + weekly collab

> Quick one. I'm playing {venue} on {nights}. Two things that cost nothing and help us both:
> 1. Listing me as a resident on your site/IG highlights (one line, "Resident: @arshia.wav"). Helps my dates show up when people search the venue.

> I'm also running a guest list through my own page ({site}/guestlist/) with a cut-off and dress code built in, and I'm happy to route table-size groups to your reservations team.
> Thanks, Arshia
